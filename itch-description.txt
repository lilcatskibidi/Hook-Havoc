AQUATIC HAVOC — Deep Sea Hunter (v1.1.0)

Cast your line into haunted waters, hook monsters, drag them ashore,
and finish them with an arsenal of ridiculous guns. Then dive deeper:
ancient bosses, a sealed void cave, a casino, and 213 catchable fish.

WHAT YOU DO
- Stand near the water, hold SPACE to charge, release to cast.
- When something bites, shoot it to drain stamina, manage line tension,
  drag it onto the sand and claim your loot.
- Sell catches, buy guns, rods, armor and bait. Talk to OLD MARLIN (E)
  for fish-catching quests.
- Slay 20 seagulls and their mother, the STORMCALLER, will come for you.
- Crack the NW-corner void portal: feed the runes fish (common, rare,
  epic), descend into the Hollow Cave, and summon bosses at the ritual
  circle — including the nine-headed STORMLORD HYDRA.

FEATURES
- 213 catchable fish across 6 rarities + 4 summonable bosses + miniboss
- 30+ guns with real rendered previews, rods, armor, craftable bait
- Boss cinematics: 10-second camera intro, war-horn roar, war-drum theme
- Deep Sea Casino (roulette, slots, fish betting, high-low)
- Fish Index, 26 achievements, LAN multiplayer, full custom-skin support
  (export/import your skins as a zip, right from Settings)
- File-first audio: drop in your own mp3/ogg/wav to replace any sound

CONTROLS
WASD move · SPACE cast / reel / pull bobber back · Mouse shoot ·
1-4 weapons · R reload · E talk / shop / portal · J fish index ·
Wheel zoom · ESC menu
