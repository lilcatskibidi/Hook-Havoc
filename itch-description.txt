AQUATIC HAVOC — Deep Sea Hunter (v1.4.0)

Cast your line into haunted waters, hook monsters, drag them ashore,
and finish them with an arsenal of ridiculous guns. Then dive deeper:
ancient bosses, a sealed void cave, a casino, wild isles, living
weather, bite-driving EVENTS — and 281 catchable fish with 32 mutations.

WHAT YOU DO
- Stand near the water, hold SPACE to charge, release to cast.
- When something bites, shoot it to drain stamina, manage line tension,
  drag it onto the sand and claim your loot.
- Dash (Q) through danger with i-frames; buy and craft consumables
  (Bandage, Adrenaline, Smoke Bomb — press F to use the equipped one).
- Sell catches, buy guns, rods, armor and bait — or CRAFT event-tide
  baits and armor from special fish + coins. Talk to OLD MARLIN (E)
  for fish-catching quests.
- Check the 📡 FISH RADAR by your spawn umbrella: live bite odds per
  species, best ferry trip, strict time/weather windows, active events.
- Slay 20 seagulls and their mother, the STORMCALLER, arrives in a 3.5s
  cutscene (storm warning, feather omen, dive landing, title card).
- Crack the SW-corner void portal: feed the runes fish (common, rare,
  epic), descend into the Hollow Cave, tear a 7-shard gate and feed the
  quartz shrine void-touched fish per rarity — including the nine-headed
  STORMLORD HYDRA and the LEVIATHAN PRIEST (blood-mutation heart chain).

FEATURES
- 281 catchable fish across 6 rarities + 32 mutations + 4 summonable bosses + miniboss
- 30+ guns with real rendered previews, rods, armor (buyable + craftable),
  craftable event baits, consumable items with hotbar + dash cooldown
- CRAFT tab (bait/armor/rod/gun sub-tabs) with key-material recipes:
  priest cores forge Heartlance, Choir Repeater, Tithe rods, Tidefather armor
- 7 rotating sky EVENTS (Typhoon, Monsoon, Fog Bank, Blood Moon, Dawn
  Chorus, Sunspell, Abyssal Surge) drive bites; strict-schedule fish
  exist ONLY in-window (e.g. 20:00–23:00 + rain) or never bite at all
- Boss cinematics: swim-in arrival, war-horn roar, 3-2-1 countdown with
  mutual freeze; bosses never flee your corpse (10 deaths and it leaves)
- Square wild isles with exact collision, lagoon lakes, buoy borders,
  one shared sea asset with the mainland, flat 1500c ferry fare
- Deep Sea Casino (roulette, slots, fish betting, high-low)
- Fish Index (auto-trimmed custom art), 26 achievements, online P2P
  multiplayer (PeerJS, same version), full custom-skin support
  (export/import your skins as a zip, right from Settings)
- Minecraft-style keybinds + sectioned Settings; every sound is a file
  in assets/audio (swap any mp3/ogg/wav to reskin it)

CONTROLS
WASD move · Q dash (dodge) · SPACE cast / reel / pull bobber back ·
Mouse shoot · 1-4 weapons · R reload · E talk / shop / portal ·
F use equipped item · J fish index · Wheel/Z/X zoom · ESC menu ·
Gamepad: stick move, RT shoot, A cast, B interact, R3 dash
