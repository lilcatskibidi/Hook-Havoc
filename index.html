About

Aquatic Havoc is a hybrid fishing/action game where you cast, hook monsters, fight them underwater, drag them to shore, and finish them off before they tear your line.

Built with pure HTML, CSS (Tailwind) and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step — just one canvas, one idea.

📱 TOUCH (MOBILE)

Left stick moves. CAST throws / reels / pulls the bobber back. FIRE auto-aims the nearest threat and shoots. Weak phones auto-drop to low perf mode.

1. Stand near the water, hold SPACE to charge, release to cast.

2. When a fish bites, shoot it to drain stamina, then hold SPACE to reel. Watch the red tension bar — ease off before it snaps!

3. Drag it onto the sand, finish it, walk over the loot to collect.

4. Sell catches, buy guns, rods, armor. Talk to OLD MARLIN (press E) for fish-catching quests.

5. Slay 20 seagulls and their mother, the STORMCALLER, will come. No fishing during her storm.

6. Time, weather and water tier matter: the clock chip (top-left) shows all three. Fish bite far more when conditions match — night hunters at night, storm fish in storms, giants in the DEEP.

7. Walk the long pier (middle of the beach) to reach SHALLOW then DEEP water — shallow glows light, deep runs dark. Ride the ⛵ ferry (real sailing trip) to 3 wild isles, each with its own pier, surrounding sea and special lake (epic / legendary / mythic bonuses). Day/Night lighting can be toggled in Settings — time keeps running.
